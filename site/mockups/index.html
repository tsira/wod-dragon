SHARED PRINCIPLES (ALL DIRECTIONS)

Glance rule: state readable in <300ms from 60cm, sweaty, mid-rep. Digits stay tabular and huge; color+shape encode state (never color alone); final-3s always gets motion + red regardless of theme. Cues (audio/haptic) are theme-flavored but structurally identical.

NOT BORING TAKEAWAYS APPLIED

Time as a single honest dimension (HP bar, plate mass, tape footage) · timer "flips" at the cap and counts overtime up (their stopwatch trick — worth adopting in the engine for capped For Time) · loud, readable, personality over chrome.
