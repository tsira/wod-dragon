THE CORE MECHANIC (ALL TONES)

Time is load. A WOD compiles to a bar: total segments = total plates. Countdown modes strip plates; count-up modes load them; the collar snaps on for the final 3 seconds; hitting a time cap doesn't stop — the bar "flips" and red overtime plates load (Not Boring's count-past-zero). Rest = bar on the ground, chalk cloud. Abort = bail the bar (plates scatter, thud).

SOUND DIRECTION SHIFT

This skin retires the square-wave beeps: foley instead. Round transition = plate clank; segment start = collar spin-click; final 3s = knurling scrape ticks; finish = the unmistakable sound of a loaded bar dropped from overhead. Haptics mirror the weight (heavier event = longer/harder pattern).
